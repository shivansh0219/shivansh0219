### 🌐 LocalLoop — Local Business & Creator Marketplace

**LocalLoop** is a web-based platform that connects **local businesses, content creators, and customers** in one place.

Businesses can create their profiles, showcase their services, publish offers, receive reviews, and collaborate with local creators for promotion.

Creators can build their profiles, showcase their portfolios, create promotional packages, and receive collaboration requests from businesses.

**Key Features:**

* 🏢 Business Registration & Login
* 👤 Customer Registration & Login
* 🎥 Creator Registration & Login
* 📋 Business Profile Management
* 📸 Business Photos & Gallery
* 🛠️ Services & Pricing Management
* 🕐 Opening Hours Management
* 🎁 Business Offers & Promotions
* ⭐ Customer Reviews & Ratings
* 📊 Business Analytics
* 🎥 Creator Profiles & Portfolios
* 📦 Creator Promotional Packages
* 🤝 Business ↔ Creator Collaboration System
* 🔄 Collaboration Status Tracking
* 💳 Subscription & Payment System
* 🔐 Role-Based Authentication
* 🛠️ Admin Business & Review Management

**Collaboration Workflow:**

```text
Business
    ↓
Send Collaboration Request
    ↓
Creator
    ↓
Accept Request
    ↓
In Progress
    ↓
Completed
```

**Technologies Used:**
